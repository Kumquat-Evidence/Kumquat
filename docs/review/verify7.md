## Overall verdict

The new content is accurate and well pitched for Step 1/2CK/3. Formulas, worked arithmetic and almost all keys are right, and the accfix corrections (non-inferiority sign convention, WHI/HERS, PERC, 45 CFR 46, period vs point prevalence, the resin outbreak numbers) are a clear improvement. Two items must be fixed before release:

- **IMR:** the "0.5%" distractor equals the key.
- **KM figure:** it is keyed and explained at 5.5 years while the drawn curve gives 6.

Three items have a second defensible answer:

- the ROC "across all cut-offs" key;
- the Anchoring bestiary scenario, which reads as diagnostic momentum;
- the Confirmation bias scenario, which overlaps anchoring and premature closure.

The rest are precision and polish issues: the forest diamond and weights, the PE threshold stem, the legion wording, the regression stem, the NNT rounding, the duplicated RTM question, the missing vault examples and the CI-before-p order. None of them would teach a wrong fact, but a strict examiner or a careful novice would notice them.
