Severity key:
- **wrong**: teaches something false.
- **ambiguous**: more than one defensible answer, or the stem can't be answered as written.
- **imprecise**: true enough for the exam, but loose or incomplete in a way that can mislead.
- **style**: wording, formatting or answer give-aways.
